## 4. Google Search Console (verify + index)

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console → **Add property** → **URL prefix** → `https://travelbillpro.pages.dev/`
   (A "Domain" property needs DNS access, which `pages.dev` doesn't give you.)
2. Choose **HTML file** verification. The file is already live at
   `https://travelbillpro.pages.dev/google6d727e955cdac399.html` → click **Verify**.
   - Cloudflare Pages redirects `….html` → `…` (same site). Google normally accepts this. If verification fails,
     choose the **HTML tag** method instead, copy the `<meta name="google-site-verification" …>` tag, and paste it
     into `index.html` just under `<!--app-head-->`. Redeploy and click Verify.
3. **Sitemaps** → submit `sitemap.xml`.
4. **URL Inspection** → request indexing for: `/`, `/travel-billing-software`, `/travel-agency-software`,
   `/travel-billing-management-software`, `/features`, `/pricing`.
5. Check **Pages**, **Core Web Vitals** and **Enhancements** (FAQ, Breadcrumbs, Software App) after 1–2 weeks.
6. **Bing Webmaster Tools** (https://www.bing.com/webmasters) → *Import from Google Search Console* (1 click).
   Bing also powers ChatGPT search and Copilot, so this matters for GEO.

## 5. Strong recommendation: use a custom domain

`*.pages.dev` works, but a brand domain (e.g. `travelbillpro.com` or `travelbillpro.in`) builds authority you own,
looks more trustworthy in search results, and is required for a working business email.
**Note:** `accounts@travelbillpro.com` is shown on the site, but `travelbillpro.com` is not registered yet, so emails to it will bounce.

When you add a domain:
1. Cloudflare Pages → Custom domains → add it.
2. Change **one line**: `url` in `src/data/site.js`. Canonicals, OG tags, schema, sitemap and robots all update.
3. Redirect `travelbillpro.pages.dev` → the new domain with a Cloudflare **Bulk Redirect** (301, preserve path).
4. In Search Console, add the new property and use **Change of Address**.

## 6. Backlink strategy

Quality beats quantity. Aim for links from **relevant** sites: software directories, Indian business listings,
travel-industry sites and accounting/GST sites. Never buy links, use PBNs or link farms; Google penalises them.

### Tier 1: Foundations (weeks 1–2, ~20 links)
**Software review directories** (also heavily cited by AI answer engines):
Capterra India · G2 · GetApp · Software Advice · SoftwareSuggest · Techjockey · SaaSworthy · GoodFirms · Crozdesk ·
AlternativeTo · Product Hunt (launch day) · SaaSHub
→ Same name, description, category ("Travel Agency Software", "Billing Software") and logo everywhere.
→ Ask your first 10 real customers to leave reviews on Capterra/G2 (real reviews only).

**Business citations (NAP must match exactly: Travel Bill Pro · Hyderabad, Telangana · +91 84569 70530):**
Google Business Profile · Bing Places · Apple Business Connect · JustDial · IndiaMART · Sulekha · TradeIndia ·
ExportersIndia · Yellow Pages India

**Social profiles** (then add the URLs to `SITE.social` in `src/data/site.js`; they appear in the footer and schema `sameAs`):
LinkedIn company page · YouTube channel · Instagram · X · Facebook page

### Tier 2: Industry authority (months 1–3)
- **Travel associations & communities:** TAAI, IATO, ADTOI, OTOAI, state taxi/tourist-vehicle operator
  associations, local transport unions. Many have member/vendor directories and newsletters.
- **Travel trade media:** TravelBizMonitor, TravTalk India, Travel Trade Journal, Express TravelWorld, Trav Trends.
  Pitch a founder story ("Hyderabad startup digitising cab billing") or a data piece.
- **GST / CA / accounting sites:** guest posts such as "GST invoicing checklist for travel agencies" on CA firm blogs,
  ClearTax-style community forums, and Tally/accounting partner blogs.
- **Partnerships:** GPS/fleet-tracking vendors, vehicle financiers, insurance agents, CA firms that serve travel clients.
  Swap "recommended tools" links and co-host webinars.

### Tier 3: Linkable assets (ongoing)
- **Free tools:** "GST invoice generator for cab operators", "per-km fare calculator", "driver salary & bata calculator".
  Free tools earn natural links and rank on their own.
- **Original data:** "State of Travel Billing in India 2026", a survey of 100 operators (billing time, payment delays).
  Journalists and bloggers link to stats.
- **Downloadable templates:** GST invoice template for travel agencies, trip sheet template, duty slip template.
- **YouTube tutorials:** "How to make a GST invoice for a cab trip", with a link back to `/travel-billing-software`.

### Tier 4: Digital PR & mentions (monthly)
- Answer journalist requests on Qwoted / Featured.com (travel tech, SMB, GST topics).
- Find unlinked brand mentions (Google Alerts for "Travel Bill Pro") and politely ask for a link.
- Helpful (non-promotional) answers on Quora, Reddit r/IndiaBusiness, and travel-operator Facebook/WhatsApp communities.

### Anchor-text guidance
Mostly brand and natural anchors ("Travel Bill Pro", "this billing tool", the URL). Use exact keywords
("travel billing software") for no more than ~10–15% of links.

| 2 | 8–12 | associations, partners, first guest posts |
| 3 | 8–12 | free tool launch, PR, YouTube |

## 7. GEO & AEO (AI search and answer engines)

Done in code:
- Every keyword page and guide opens with a **direct, quotable answer** ("Travel billing software is…").
- FAQ sections with **FAQPage schema** on all keyword pages, guides and pricing.
- **Prerendered HTML**, because ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity crawlers don't run JavaScript.
- `robots.txt` explicitly allows GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, Bingbot.
- `llms.txt` summarises the product, pricing and key URLs for LLMs.
- Consistent entity facts (name, location, phone, pricing) across the site and schema.

To do (off-site; this matters most for AI citations):
- Get listed and reviewed on **G2, Capterra and SoftwareSuggest**. AI assistants cite these heavily for "best X software" questions.
- Keep the same one-line description everywhere: *"Travel Bill Pro is travel billing and travel agency software for India."*
- Publish YouTube videos with clear titles and transcripts.
- Every month, ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot "best travel billing software in India" and note whether you're mentioned.

## 9. Before you launch: content you must replace

- **Testimonials and client names are samples.** Replace them in `src/data/content.js` with real, approved customers.
  Fake reviews violate Google's spam policies and consumer law. No Review/Rating schema is added until reviews are real.
- **Email:** `accounts@travelbillpro.com` won't work until the domain exists (see section 5).
- **Social profile URLs:** add them to `src/data/site.js` once the profiles are created.
- **Stats** (500+ businesses, ₹10Cr+ billed…) must reflect real numbers.

## 10. Measuring progress

- Google Search Console: impressions/clicks for the 3 target keywords (Performance → Queries).
- PageSpeed Insights (https://pagespeed.web.dev): aim for green Core Web Vitals on mobile.
- Rich Results Test (https://search.google.com/test/rich-results): validate FAQ, Breadcrumb, SoftwareApplication.
- Schema validator (https://validator.schema.org).
- Track referring domains monthly (Ahrefs Webmaster Tools and Bing Webmaster are free for your own site).

Ranking for competitive terms like "travel billing software" typically takes **3–6 months** of consistent content and
link building. Long-tail terms and branded searches usually move first.
